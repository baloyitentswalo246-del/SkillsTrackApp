Privacy

Last Updated: October 2026

SkillsTrack is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and secure your data within the Firebase database infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect
 We collect information necessary to provide effective learner support:
Profile Information: Your display name, email address, and assigned role.
Task Data: Learning tasks, categories, priorities, due dates, and completion status managed through the Task Manager.
Support Bookings: Topics, preferred dates, notes, and status updates for requested support sessions
Activity Metrics: Mini-game scores and learning progress summaries.

2. How We Protect Your Data
Data Isolation: User records are securely structured under individual user IDs.
Firebase Security Rules ensure that users can only read and write data associated with their own authenticated identity.
Authentication Security: User authentication is managed securely via Firebase,
ensuring passwords are encrypted and never exposed in the database or source code.
No Unrestricted Access: Public write access is strictly disabled via database security rules.

3. How We Use Your Information
Authenticate your identity and maintain your active session.
Track learning progress, calculate completion rates, and display dynamic dashboards.
Facilitate support bookings between learners and assessors.
Record mini-game outcomes and performance feedback.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
Your data remains stored in the Firebase Realtime Database for the duration of your enrolment or active project cycle.
You may request updates or deletion of your task and preference data directly through the application interface or by contacting your system administrator.
